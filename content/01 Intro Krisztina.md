This is the age of Landfall

Generations ago, your ancestors fled their homeland in haste, crossing the ocean aboard ships built with little time and fewer provisions. They sailed east, leaving behind a calamity so terrible that those who survived have rarely spoken of it since.

You were born on the continent your people now call Axandor Tor.

The generations that followed knew only fragments of the old stories. The calamity itself became little more than a whispered legend, and the reasons for your ancestors' flight were buried alongside those who remembered them. Now, only a handful of elders remain who made the crossing themselves, and they guard the truth of what happened in the old world closely.

Fifty years ago, your people founded a city on these shores and named it Dawnport—a symbol of a new beginning after the darkness they had left behind.

Since then, humanity has pushed steadily inland, exploring and settling the unfamiliar lands of Axandor Tor. Along the way, they encountered the dwarves, with whom they forged a lasting alliance against the giants.

But not every encounter has been peaceful.

For generations, humans have been locked in a brutal war with the giants. What began as conflict over territory has grown into a struggle that threatens to consume the frontier.

Now, as Dawnport continues to grow and humanity ventures ever deeper into Axandor Tor, the old world has become a distant memory.
