## **Draevan The Chainmaker**
(Lawfull Evil)  
Slavery · Obedience · Punishment · Cruelty 
Worshippers:  Slaveholders, jailers, inquisitors, cruel rulers
Moto: *“Freedom is merely the absence of a master. Give me your chains, and I will give you purpose.”*

Lesser Powers:
* **Kareth, The Warden** — Prisons, jailers, confinement and imprisonment.  
* **Vessa, The Taskmistress** — Slavery, forced labor and exploitation.

Spirits:
* **The Black Magistrate** — Punishment without mercy.  
* **The Chain** — Obedience, submission and subjugation.  
* **The Lash** — Physical punishment and pain.
