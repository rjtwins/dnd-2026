What did your character do?

[[Emi Kurose]] was a navigator and protector at a small monastery on the southern islands, which kept a shrine to Laavah, the Seeker. I guided pilgrims and travelers between sacred sites, reading the stars, winds, tides, and currents to keep them safe.

Who were they?

I was Emi Kurose, a wood elf and devoted follower of [[Laavah The Seeker]]. I believed every journey had something new waiting beyond the horizon, and that everyone deserved to reach their destination safely. As the civil war spread, I continued escorting travelers regardless of their allegiance.

How did they die?

Near the end of the war, our ship was caught in a sudden, violent storm after news reached us that the imperial capital had fallen. I got the pilgrims and crew into smaller boats and stayed behind to keep the ship from being driven onto the rocks.

They survived. I did not.

One pilgrim had been carrying a mysterious document that he guarded carefully. I never learned what it contained—or whether it had anything to do with the storm.
