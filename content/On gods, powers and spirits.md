# The upper pantheon:

## **Aureth The Sovereign**:
(Lawfull Neutral)  
Leadership · Law · Order · War  
Worshipped by Nobles, Magistrates, Soldiers, City dwellers  

Note: They are considered by their worshippers to be the leader of the gods, although this is not necessarily shared by followers of other gods.

Motto: *“Order is the foundation upon which civilization stands. Without those who rule, there is only chaos.”*

Lesser powers:
* **Veyra, Keeper of Oaths** — Oaths, contracts, promises and sworn loyalty.  
* **Caedran, The Just Hand** — Justice, courts, judges and fair judgment.  
* **Ordan, The Standard-Bearer** — Armies, military discipline, banners and soldiers.  
* **Marath, The Crowned** — Kingship, nobility, legitimate rule and succession.  
* **Halvek, The Lawgiver** — Written law, bureaucracy, codes and civic administration.
Spirits:
* **The Iron Judge** — A mysterious divine figure said to appear when a ruler must make an impossible judgment.  
* **The First Soldier** — The ideal of the soldier who obeys orders while accepting responsibility for their actions.
## **Laavah The Seeker**,  
(Chaotic neutral)  
Youth · Song · Travel · Mystery · Discovery  
Worshipped by Adventurers, Bards, Musicians, Scholars.

Motto: *“There is always another road, another song, another secret waiting beyond the horizon. Go and find it.”*

Lesser powers:
* **Miralen, The First Bard** — Music, poetry, inspiration and performance.  
* **Tavian, The Traveler** — Roads, travelers, wandering and journeys.  
* **Elyra, The Unanswered Question** — Mysteries, riddles, curiosity and unanswered questions.  
* **Perrin, The Open Door** — Opportunity, beginnings, adventure and unexpected chances.  
* **Sella, The Young Heart** — Youth, optimism, play and youthful courage.
Spirits:
* **The Wandering Star** — Exploration and journeys into unknown lands.  

* **The Nameless Child** — Curiosity, innocence and the desire to understand the world.
## **Vael The End**,
(True Neutral)  
Death · Dying · Funerals · Passing On  
Worshipped by, The dying, Terminal ill, Soldiers, Those in mortal danger.

Lesser Powers:
* **Edris, The Ferryman** — Guides the dead toward their final destination.  
* **Mora, The Mourner** — Grief, mourning and remembrance.  
* **Sethren, The Grave Keeper** — Graves, cemeteries, tombs and those who tend them.  
* **Alen, The Last Breath** — The final moments of life and peaceful death.
Spirits:
* **The Pale Midwife** — Death during childbirth and the strange boundary between life and death. 
* **The Black Hound** — Guardian of graves and hunter of those who attempt to escape death.
## **Liora The Gentle** **Flame**,
(Chaotic Good) 
Life · Light · Healing  
Worshipped by, Parents, Docters, Healers

Motto: *“Life is fragile, but it is precious. Where there is suffering, bring healing; where there is darkness, bring light.”*

Lesser Powers:
* **Alira, The Midwife** — Birth, pregnancy, mothers and newborn children.  
* **Theron, The Physician** — Medicine, doctors, surgery and healing.  
* **Solenne, The Hearth-Mother** — Family, hospitality, warmth and home.  
* **Mariel, The Kindly Light** — Compassion, charity and care for the helpless.  
* **Evin, The Greenhand** — Plants, agriculture, fertility and growth.
Spirits:
* **The Golden Stag** — Vitality, nature and healthy life.  
* **The Little Flame** — Household protection, lamps, hearths and keeping a home alive.
## **Serathiel The Cleansing Flame**
(Lawfull Good)  
Light · Fire · Purity
Worshippers, Paladins, Soldiers, Judges

Motto: *“What is pure must be protected, and what is corrupt must be burned away. Fire does not negotiate with rot.”*

Lesser Powers:
* **Avariel, The White Sword** — Holy warriors, righteous warfare and defense against corruption.  
* **Cyran, The Flamekeeper** — Sacred fire, temples and religious ritual.  
* **Ilyra, The Unblemished** — Virtue, innocence, chastity and moral purity.
Spirits:
* **The Burning Eye** — Truth, revelation and exposing corruption.  
* **The Ashen Hand** — Purification through destruction.  
* **The Sword of Dawn** — The battle against darkness, demons and supernatural evil.
## **Thalor The Shieldarm**
(Neutral Good)  
Protection · Safety · Preservation
Motto: *“Stand between the innocent and the blade. Let it break upon you before it reaches them.”*

Lesser Powers:
* **Brenna, The Gatekeeper** — Gates, walls, fortifications and city defenses.  
* **Hadrik, The Shieldbearer** — Soldiers who defend rather than conquer.  
* **Myrra, The Hearthguard** — Homes, families and domestic safety.  
* **Oren, The Watchman** — Vigilance, guards, sentries and watchmen.
Spirits:
* **The Last Sentinel** — Those who stand guard when everyone else has fled.  
* **The Old Oak** — Endurance, shelter and protection from the elements.
# **The lower pantheon**
## **Varkhan The Usurper**
(Lawfull Evil)  
Domination · Conquest · Power · Ambition    
Worshippers:  Tyrants, conquerors, despots, ambitious nobles
Motto: *"I was born to rule. The only question is whether I have the strength to take what is mine."*

Lesser Powers:
* **Zareth, The Conqueror** — Military conquest, empire and victory.  
* **Velka, The Iron Crown** — Absolute monarchy and unquestioned authority.  
* **Damar, The Victor** — Competition, triumph and defeating one's rivals.  
* **Arveth, The Ambitious** — Personal ambition, advancement and ruthless success.
Spirits:
* **The Red Throne** — The divine embodiment of power and rulership.  
* **The Usurped King** — Those who seize a position that was never rightfully theirs.  
* **The (First) Conqueror** — The mythical ancestor emperor who built their empire through conquest.
## **Draevan The Chainmaker**
(Lawfull Evil)  
Slavery · Obedience · Punishment · Cruelty 
Worshippers:  Slaveholders, jailers, inquisitors, cruel rulers
Moto: *“Freedom is merely the absence of a master. Give me your chains, and I will give you purpose.”*

Lesser Powers:
* **Kareth, The Warden** — Prisons, jailers, confinement and imprisonment.  
* **Vessa, The Taskmistress** — Slavery, forced labor and exploitation.
Spirits:
* **The Black Magistrate** — Punishment without mercy.  
* **The Chain** — Obedience, submission and subjugation.  
* **The Lash** — Physical punishment and pain.
## **Nymara The Whisper**
(Neutral Evil)  
Secrets · Deception · Intrigue · Betrayal  
Worshippers:  Spies, criminals, conspirators, assassins
Moto: *“Truth is a weapon. A wise hand does not draw it until the moment it will do the most harm.”*

Lesser Powers:
* **Velis, The Masked** — Disguises, false identities and impersonation.  
* **Sareth, The Listener** — Spies, surveillance and eavesdropping.
Spirits:
* **The Faceless One** — Anonymity, disguises and disappearing without a trace.  
* **The Black Tongue** — Lies, propaganda and persuasive deception.  
* **The Spider** — Conspiracies, manipulation and elaborate schemes.
## **Morveth The Rotting**
 (Chaotic Evil)  
Decay · Disease · Corruption · Entropy  
Worshippers:  Necromancers, plague cults, the desperate.  
Moto: “Everything that lives will rot. Everything that is built will crumble. I merely hasten what was always coming.”

Lesser Powers:
* **Nhalia, The Plague-Mother** — Disease, epidemics and infection.  
* **Gorath, The Worm-King** — Corpses, worms and decomposition.
Spirits:
* **The Carrion Crow** — Scavengers and creatures that feed upon the dead.  
* **The Corpse Tree** — Undeath rooted in the physical world.  
* **The Hollow Man** — A person whose body continues functioning after their soul has departed.
# On Saints and other chosen
**SAINTS ARE A WORK IN PROGRESS**
Saints (and other chosen) are the living embodiment of a God's will and power, they are chosen individuals, often powerful paladins.

The powers and missions of a saint vary on a case by case basis, some may be near immortal demi gods while others may be hermits dispensing wisdom.

## Known saints in 803 IE:

### Saint Maera, The Keeper of the Dying (Veal)
 *Living (born sometime 534 IE)*
A very old woman who lives in a hospice outside **Dawnport**. She cares for those who have no family and sits with the dying so that nobody has to face death alone.
She claims that Vael has never spoken to her.
She may be telling the truth.

### Saint Alwen (Liora)
*Dead — 447 IE*
A healer who supposedly cured hundreds of children during a devastating plague. She continued treating the sick after becoming infected herself, upon her death divine intervention ended the plague.

Hospitals and orphanages frequently carry her name.

### Saint Serin, The Gentle Hand (Liora)
 *Living (Born sometime 741 IE)*
A traveling physician who refuses payment from the poor. He has become famous for treating soldiers on both sides of wars.

This has caused some political problems.
He claims that:
*“Liora does not ask which banner a wounded man carried.”*

### Saint Avarra, The White Sword (Serathiel)
 *Dead — 318 IE*
A paladin who led a campaign against a powerful cult that had infiltrated several cities.

She destroyed the cult completely, but afterward voluntarily surrendered her sword and spent the remainder of her life investigating whether her own methods had gone too far.

She is revered by both warriors and inquisitors.

### Saint Caldris, The Undying Flame (Serathiel)
 *Living (Born sometime 780 IE)*
A powerful paladin who has survived wounds that should have killed him several times.

His devotion to Serathiel is absolute, in her name he cleanses evil and corruption wherever they may appear.

Founder and head of The Order of The Divine Light

### Saint Callian, The Farwalker, the eye of Laavah.
 *Dead — 391 IE*
An adventurer who spent forty years exploring the unknown regions of the world. He supposedly discovered seven lost civilizations and returned from places no map had recorded.

His final journey ended when he disappeared into an unexplored mountain range.
His last words were supposedly:
*“There is something beyond this mountain.”*

Nobody knows what he found.
