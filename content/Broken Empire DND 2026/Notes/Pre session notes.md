Ugly Goblin, a goblin with exceptionally keen senses, made her living performing whatever services people would pay for—from investigating unfaithful lovers and gathering political dirt to finding kidnapped children. Eventually, her work led her to hold someone at blade point. When the target refused to surrender a stolen family heirloom, she killed them.

Afterward, a strange second voice began speaking within her. Though it sounded like someone else, it also felt disturbingly like her own voice. On her worst nights, the voice could even wrestle control of her body. Ugly Goblin had long accepted herself as nothing more than a monster, but the things this voice told her began to make her question that belief.

Then a mysterious client hired her to uncover the whereabouts of Axandor Tor's most beautiful princesses. Her investigation revealed that one princess had made a pact with an otherworldly being through an ancient artifact. When Ugly Goblin accidentally activated the artifact, the being manifested and offered her beauty beyond measure.

For a moment, she hesitated. The price was another person's soul, damned to eternal torment. Before she could decide, however, the second voice seized control of her body and commanded the being to sacrifice **Ugly Goblin's own soul instead**.

The world went dark and Ugly Goblin died.
