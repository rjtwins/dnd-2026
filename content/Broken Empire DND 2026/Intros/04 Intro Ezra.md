This is the Age of Empire

Generations ago, humanity came to Axandor Tor as refugees.

They survived crossing the ocean, built Dawnport upon its shores, and slowly spread across a continent that was never meant to be theirs. They fought the giants alongside the dwarves, raised kingdoms, and spent centuries warring among themselves.

Then came the conqueror.

The human kingdoms were united beneath the Empire, and for generations the imperial banner has continued to spread across Axandor Tor.

Now, the Empire stands at the height of its power.

To the south, imperial armies have driven the orc and other savage tribes from the fertile borderlands. Those who survived have been pushed deep into the Ashwaste Badlands, their ancestral lands claimed for farms, cities, and imperial roads. The southern frontier, once considered too dangerous to settle, now feeds the heart of the Empire.

To the north, a darker conquest unfolds.

For generations, the Empire and the elves lived in an uneasy peace.

What followed was a brutal war unlike anything seen since the age of the giants. Imperial armies marched into the northern realms, and one elven kingdom after another fell. Forests were burned, ancient cities destroyed, and entire populations were displaced.

The elven realms are broken.

Only the Elven Islands remain beyond imperial control, their shores guarded by the last remnants of a people who once ruled the north.

Those elves who remain within the Empire live as subjects rather than equals. Many have been enslaved, their ancient kingdoms reduced to memory. Worse still, something has changed within them.

The elves were once a people who did not know the frailty of mortal life. Now they age and no one knows why. Some blame the Empire. Others believe the answer lies in the ancient magic of the elven realms.

The dwarves remain allies of the Empire, though even they have begun to question the direction their ancient alliance has taken.

And the Empire continues to expand.

Its roads stretch farther than ever before. Its cities grow richer. Its armies are vast, disciplined, and victorious. Peoples from across Axandor Tor now live beneath the imperial banner, bound together by law, conquest, and necessity.

The Empire brought an end to the wars that once divided humanity.
It has brought civilization to lands once considered wild.
It has brought peace to the heartlands.
It has never been larger.
Its enemies have never been weaker.
For now, the Empire stands unchallenged.

Its people believe its power will endure, that the Empire will stand for a thousand years.
They do not know that they are living through the height of its power.
