This is the age of Kingdoms

Centuries have passed since humanity first came to Axandor Tor.

Long ago, your ancestors fled their homeland across the sea, escaping a calamity so terrible that its true nature was lost to time. They arrived as strangers on an unfamiliar continent, built Dawnport upon its shores, and began the long struggle to make Axandor Tor their home.

Much has changed since those first days.

The war between humanity and the giants raged for generations, but in time, the humans and dwarves stood together and defeated their ancient foes. Their alliance, forged in blood and battle, became the foundation of a new era. With the giants broken, the peoples of Axandor Tor finally began to look beyond the frontiers that had consumed them for centuries.

Humanity spread across the continent, founding kingdoms, cities, and roads where once there had been only wilderness. Rival crowns now compete for land, influence, and wealth, but for the first time, humans have begun to think of Axandor Tor not as a frontier, but as their home.

And humanity is no longer alone.

Beyond the northern reaches lie the lands of the elves. After generations of isolation, contact between the two peoples has opened a new chapter in the continent's history. Trade, diplomacy, and travel have brought humans and elves closer together, and a tentative peace now holds between the races. The dwarves, meanwhile, remain steadfast allies of humanity, their shared history binding them together despite the inevitable tensions of a changing world.

For the first time in centuries, Axandor Tor knows something resembling peace.

Yet peace brings its own uncertainties.

Old kingdoms now jostle against new ones. Ancient peoples are being drawn into one another's affairs. The scars left by the giant wars have not fully healed, and beyond the borders of civilization lie lands still largely unknown. Meanwhile, the oldest stories—the ones whispered by the few who still remember the journey across the sea—have faded almost entirely from memory.

The old world is little more than a legend now.
