(Lawfull Good)  
Light · Fire · Purity
Paladins, Soldiers, Judges

Motto: *“What is pure must be protected, and what is corrupt must be burned away. Fire does not negotiate with rot.”*

Lesser Powers:
* **Avariel, The White Sword** — Holy warriors, righteous warfare and defense against corruption.  
* **Cyran, The Flamekeeper** — Sacred fire, temples and religious ritual.  
* **Ilyra, The Unblemished** — Virtue, innocence, chastity and moral purity.

Spirits:
* **The Burning Eye** — Truth, revelation and exposing corruption.  
* **The Ashen Hand** — Purification through destruction.  
* **The Sword of Dawn** — The battle against darkness, demons and supernatural evil.

Organization:
Most major cities or towns will have a temple to Serathiel.
Sarathiel priesthood is organized and unified.
There are many martial organizations dedicated to Sarathiel
