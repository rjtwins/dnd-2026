The Order of The Devine Light is a "holy" order of paladins.
Their headquarters are just outside of Dawnport.
They are lead by [[On gods, powers and spirits#Saint Caldris, The Undying Flame (Serathiel)|Saint Caldris]]

They are considered "fanatics" tolerating little in terms of evil and corruption.
Generally considered "good guys" they however are looked at wearily when entering Dawnport itself by the locals.

The order is currently busy with its war against the green skin invaders occupying the broken seat.
