This agency was created in the decades following the Conquerors rise and the establishment of the empire.

The office ensures loyalty of the various factions, noble families and organizations inside the empire.
Its activities range from manipulation to out right elimination of deemed threads external or internal.

The office made extensive use of magics to create doublegangers. 

The office was instrumental in defeating the giants, in a vile ritual they took the giants powers and sealed them. This power was later used to "empower" certain chosen agents.

In the years preceding the civil war the office grew in power and its measures became more and more severe in a desperate attempt to keep the empire together. 
Ultimately the agency fractured after its director was assassinated its various successor factions backing different warlords in the civil war period.

A splinter faction of the old organization now runs the Academy in Spell hold.
